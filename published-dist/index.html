The idea behind DHAGO

Made of small, thoughtful things.

Dhago means thread in Nepali. Here, chapters become woven swatches, joined by the simple line of a film. A familiar play button, a clear clock, a place for every control. Character without a new set of rules.

Room for the picture

Controls rest on a solid shelf below the film. Fine textures carry the chapter rhythm; a single, clear thumb keeps time. Your video gets the space it deserves.

Take your own pace

Use a keyboard, turn on descriptive captions, change speed or try a narrow player. Motion preferences are respected. No account needed to watch.
