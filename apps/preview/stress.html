Beyond the studio

Every screen tells a different story.

Keep the film playing above as you explore. Some moments happen beyond this studio: watching a live broadcast, enjoying the film on an iPhone, sharing it with a larger screen or following along with a screen reader.

  1. Live, with room to rewind. Try a real broadcast and, when it allows, move back to an earlier moment.
  2. From your phone to the big screen. Turn your iPhone sideways, enjoy fullscreen and try AirPlay with a compatible television.
  3. Watch your own way. Enlarge the page, use a keyboard or touch, and explore the film with a screen reader.

We would rather leave room for your experience than pretend to know how every screen will feel.

Playback and privacy

DHAGO works without connecting another player. For optional comparisons, a compatible JavaScript integration runs inside a separate, restricted frame. An unavailable integration is marked Not assessed, not counted as a playback failure. Files you choose stay on this device, although third-party code can make its own network requests.
