Behind the study

A small film.
A considered frame.

The film uses original generated documentary imagery and original narration. Its four equal chapters follow a truthful material progression: raw allo fibre, hand preparation, spinning into thread and warp, then warp-and-weft weaving into finished cloth.

The player carries that structure into the timeline. Each chapter becomes a distinct woven swatch. A conventional seek thumb, a readable chapter name and controls on a solid shelf let the texture stay expressive while the film remains easy to navigate.

This journal uses the same portable DHAGO skin as the player collection, in an Ink treatment with a gold accent. The film, chapter data and controls are shared; the surrounding reading experience is specific to this page.
