## AI and owner contract

AI authors nearly all implementation, tests, docs, release files and form copy. The 99% target concerns authored deliverables, not measured total effort. Owner tasks: account identity/authentication, rights confirmation, visual feedback, physical-phone and assistive-technology checks, terms acceptance, final entry authorization. Never accept terms or claim submission on the owner's behalf without that authorization. Continue independent work around account blockers.

## Time and money

24-32 active agent hours and 60-120 owner minutes are planning estimates. Example 28h allocation: design 4h, implementation 10h, QA 7h, packaging/deployment 3h, contingency 4h. No paid purchase is authorized. Target zero incremental spend using existing AI access and free hosting allowances; obtain agreement before optional spending up to the plan's $20 ceiling. Record actual usage and free-tier limits; no guarantee of zero future costs.

## Definition of done

Public working player, portable skin source, tested release, captured visual, accurate description, truthful optional bonus evidence, and observed submission receipt. This repository's checklist tracks evidence, not prize probability. A blocker in Mux or final entry does not prevent building the player or preparing assets.
