# Independent semantic adjudication execution protocol

This protocol does not assert any human has performed an assessment. The generated 600-case package includes 500 public external test-group cases (150 AmbiEnt and 350 TempAmbiQA) and 100 separate model-authored family challenges (five distinct questions per family; structural scenario groups can repeat and are explicitly counted). Existing public annotation is not new human adjudication. Additional genuinely new scenarios must be elicited independently for contamination-resistant final acceptance.

1. An operator enrolls three distinct externally verified Ed25519 public keys (two reviewers and a separate adjudicator), and retains an independent identity/conflict-of-interest and timestamp record. Never share private keys or generate pretend reviewers.
2. Send only the blind packet independently to both reviewers, withholding restricted prediction/reference files and the other reviewer’s annotations. Each reviews plausible readings, primary family, ambiguity or uncertainty, rationale and actual elapsed milliseconds, and signs the exact UTF-8 payload. Preserve its digest and signature before revealing the frozen prediction.
3. Reveal only the matching prediction after both blind receipts are locked. Each reviewer signs a prediction-review receipt bound to their own blind receipt, the exact case and prediction. Classifier-only rows have no generated meanings and cannot establish resolution accuracy; keep resolution UNRESOLVED.
4. A separate adjudicator receives both blind and prediction-review receipts and reference material, resolves disagreement if supported, and signs the final receipt. UNSURE is not a negative label. Use the measured elapsed time to estimate burden; timestamps and elapsed time are externally attested/self-reported, not automatically verified.
5. Verify with verifySemanticAdjudication in semantic-human-receipts.js. The exact schemas are defined in that module and its fixture tests. Signatures establish enrolled key control, not human identity, independent authorship, trusted timestamp or actual blinding. The numerical readiness gate still requires independent operator attestation and scenario-grouped uncertainty. Do not pool synthetic-origin and external/new scenarios to conceal failures.

Artifacts named *blind* may be shared with reviewers; *predictions-restricted* and *reference-restricted* must not be shared before the appropriate stage. Human review is currently pending.
