## Re-open procedure

When a human becomes available:
1. identify only the task/role needed;
2. confirm participation/authorization before collecting data or initiating paid/external activity;
3. use the task-specific consent/privacy protocol;
4. de-identify public evidence unless identity is explicitly required by the competition;
5. keep raw private records outside the public repository;
6. record the actual result, including zero/failed participation where applicable.
