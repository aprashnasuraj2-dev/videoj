## Eligibility/exclusion facts that affect SUTRA

### Entrant eligibility
- Individual entrants must be at least the age of majority where they reside at entry.
- Teams and organizations may enter; a team/organization must appoint an authorized human Representative who itself meets the eligibility requirements.
- Eligible individuals may participate in more than one team/organization and may also enter individually.
- Sponsor/Partner employees who otherwise qualify are treated as Employee Entrants and are eligible only for the Employee Prize.
- A team/organization containing an Employee Entrant is treated as an Employee Entrant for prize eligibility.

### Geographic/affiliation exclusions
The rules exclude:
- residents/organizations in jurisdictions where U.S. or local law prohibits participation or receipt of a prize; the rules list examples including Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran and North Korea and comprehensively sanctioned OFAC jurisdictions;
- organizations involved in design, production, paid promotion, execution or distribution of the Hackathon (“Promotion Entities”);
- except for the expressly permitted Employee Entrant category, employees/representatives/agents of Promotion Entities and specified immediate-family/household relationships.

No conclusion about the entrant is made here; residence, age, affiliation and representative status remain private participant inputs.

## Project/submission conditions relevant to existing code

- The Project must be a working open agentic website using standard open-web technologies and conform to the open agentic web specification published as of the start of the Submission Period.
- It must be publicly accessible, run consistently in a current browser and function as depicted/described.
- Existing projects are permitted only if they were **significantly updated after the Submission Period began**, and the entrant should explain those updates.
- Third-party SDK/API/data use must be authorized under applicable terms/licenses.
- The public site must provide a conforming agent-network JSON file, a browser runtime and an interaction interface. LLM/key configuration is optional.
- Submission materials require a text description, screenshots and a public YouTube demonstration.
- The demonstration should be under two minutes, show the Project running in a browser and include the Entrant explaining it.
- The video must not include third-party trademarks, copyrighted music or other protected material without permission.
- Testing access must be free of charge and unrestricted for Sponsor/Administrator/Judges through the Judging Period.
- Submission materials must be English or accompanied by English translations.

## Ownership / pre-existing / open-source conditions

The rules require the Submission to be the entrant/team/organization's original work product, solely owned by the entrant/team/organization and non-infringing.

The rules expressly permit use of open-source software or hardware provided applicable licenses are followed and the submission creates software that enhances/builds on the underlying open-source product.

This makes the technical boundary in `OWNERSHIP_BOUNDARY.md` necessary but not sufficient: that inventory identifies reused versus SUTRA-period code; it does not establish legal ownership or permission.

## Financial/preferential-support exclusion

The rules state that a Project must not have been developed, or derived from a Project developed, with financial or preferential support from the Sponsor or Administrator before the end of the Submission Period. Examples include funding/investment, contract development or commercial licensing from the Sponsor/Administrator. Sponsor discretion also applies to real/apparent conflicts of interest.

Employee Entrants may not submit a project developed as part of their job duties or using Sponsor/Partner resources unavailable to all entrants.

## Entrant-specific questions still unresolved

Before phase 001 can close, the participant must privately establish:

1. age-of-majority status and country/territory of residence at entry;
2. whether the submission is individual, team or organization and, if applicable, the authorized Representative;
3. whether any entrant is a Sponsor/Partner/Promotion-Entity employee, representative, agent, immediate-family or household member under the rule definitions;
4. whether any entrant/project has disqualifying Sponsor/Administrator financial or preferential support or an apparent conflict;
5. rights/ownership basis for pre-existing DHAGO code used by SUTRA and any third-party contributions;
6. rights to the eventual lesson footage, narration, written guide, logos/marks, music and screenshots/video material;
7. whether every third-party SDK/API/data integration is permitted under its applicable terms;
8. whether the final public site/testing access will remain free and unrestricted through judging;
9. whether the final demo narrator is the actual entrant/authorized representative required for the submission narrative.

These questions are recorded as blockers/inputs only. Their answers should not be committed if doing so would expose private identity or documents.
