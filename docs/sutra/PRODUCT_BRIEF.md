# Initial SUTRA learner task

Primary audience: a field technician reviewing a short training video while preparing a handover record. Initial job: find the lesson's optical-power recording step, compare its instructions with the written checklist, and save a source-linked learning card.

Manual baseline: scrub the film, search transcript text, open the checklist and manually transcribe references. Friction hypotheses are missed timestamps, inconsistent versions and time spent switching sources; these are not measured findings yet.

Input: “Where does this lesson show the power-reading record step, and what should the handover card contain?” Only project-owned/rights-cleared lesson and checklist content may be used publicly. Until supplied, all development data is explicitly synthetic.

Output: exact video cue and source version; separate written-checklist excerpt; visible agreement, conflict or missing information; a human-reviewed local card. No inferred agreement or operational success is labeled verified.

Three failure examples: (1) seek goes to a cue from an older film; (2) model invents a checklist requirement; (3) film and written procedure disagree but the answer hides the conflict. The validator handles reference/identity integrity; semantic agreement and safe training interpretation still require human review.

Task success: the user can open a valid matching cue, inspect the relevant written text, see any conflict and confirm a card containing those actual references. Measure completion, time to valid evidence, errors and assistance against manual search. No improvement, competence, certification or operational safety outcome is assumed.

Assumptions: English is complete for judges; Nepali labels are planned. Initial use is a current desktop/mobile browser with intermittent/slow connectivity and a transcript-first fallback. Reading a transcript does not require live model access. Users understand basic technician vocabulary; this is a hypothesis to test.

Unresolved: the participant must appoint a competent factual reviewer and provide/authorize original lesson material. No reviewer identity or permission has been invented. This prevents closing P002-07 and phase 002, but not synthetic development or the standalone validator.
