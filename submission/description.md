DHAGO treats video time as a woven ribbon: chapter intervals become tactile swatches, with a real chapter readout and a conventional seek thumb. Familiar controls rest on a solid shelf below the picture, bringing a textile-inspired identity to documentary and craft films. Linen, Ink and Paper share the same interface; keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior are built into the skin. Built with Video.js v10.
