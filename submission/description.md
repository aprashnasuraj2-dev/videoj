DHAGO turns video time into a woven ribbon: chapters become distinct coloured inlays, while exposed stream state can leave an honest viewing history—rendition thickness, buffering slack and dropped-frame stitches—without inventing unavailable telemetry. Drawing from the crossing threads, restrained colour and finished edges of Nepali weaving, it gives the player tactile character without distracting from the film. Built with Video.js v10, the same responsive skin provides real play/pause, volume, captions, speed, fullscreen and keyboard controls across Linen, Ink and Paper treatments. The original 64-second motion study plays from Mux and can also be explored through scene notes in the public Studio Journal.

The portable adoption path is documented in `INSTALL.md` with HTML/Vite, React and supported theme-token examples. A host-neutral ribbon component and adapter-based Stress Lab are published alongside the skin so the timeline and edge-case harness can be reused outside DHAGO.
