## 60-second judge path

1. **0–8s:** land on the player during active playback. The woven chapter ribbon is immediately recognizable as time, not decoration.
2. **8–20s:** drag the ribbon into chapter three and see real seek state, thumbnail/time preview and selected chapter remain aligned.
3. **20–31s:** switch Linen → Ink → Paper or resize to phone width. The same controls and semantics remain usable.
4. **31–43s:** show the finished-cloth state and chapter map, including nonvisual chapter semantics.
5. **43–53s:** open Stress Lab and show either the 240px host or the external-adapter browser loader.
6. **53–60s:** show layered weft or the reusable ribbon proof, then return to the public Studio Journal/Mux playback.

This is a suggested review path, **not** a claim that an actual judge has performed it.

## Engineering tradeoffs

- Do not replace the original authored 64-second film with external footage for the lead capture; external footage is limited to clearly credited stress cases.
- Do not infer bitrate from decoded dimensions.
- A same-resolution, different-bitrate rendition pair is the counterexample that prevents that inference.
- Speaker/sponsor/custom metadata is only shown when explicitly supplied and fixture data is labeled as fixture data.
- Do not claim a two-hour geometry check is an actually decoded two-hour film.
- Do not claim live/DVR without a real live source and real seekable bounds.
- Do not claim physical iOS/Android, Safari/macOS, VoiceOver or NVDA testing unless genuinely performed.
- Do not turn Stress Lab or the ribbon proof into separate competition entries.

## Final gate

Only after the exact public build passes typecheck, build, Chromium, Firefox/WebKit-engine proof, accessibility gates, public route verification, Mux playback verification, final evidence capture and owner review should any contest entry be submitted.
