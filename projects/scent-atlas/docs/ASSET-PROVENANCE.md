# Asset provenance and depiction boundaries

**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Project:** Scent Atlas (self-initiated fictional portfolio work)

## Original editorial scene studies

The three photographs used by this static experience were created in this conversation using AI image generation for this specific fictional design brief, not downloaded from or licensed from third-party photo libraries. The request described a rain-slick stone surface, warm cedar shavings, and a torn orange peel as a continuous triptych. The approved source output was divided into three editorial panels and converted to project-local WebP. The assets are illustrative and must not be represented as documentation of real perfume ingredients, product formulation or real retail stock.
