## Copy-ready post (approx. 115 words)

Wet pavement. A cedar workshop. A freshly peeled orange.

A familiar smell needs very little explanation.

I built **Scent Atlas**, a self-initiated concept for a perfume store that starts with places people already know, not a wall of fragrance terminology. Choose a scene, read the feeling in plain language, compare six imagined scents, and discover a three-vial sample kit designed around one lead and two thoughtful contrasts.

The entire journey is linkable, works without a mandatory quiz, and ends transparently: this is a concept demo, not a live order.

Explore the experience: https://scent-atlas.aafnaipatro.com/
