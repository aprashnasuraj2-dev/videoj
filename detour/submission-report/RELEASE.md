## Handoff sequence

- Verify the public URL from a signed-out browser on the frozen source revision. Show a genuine position change, a return and the five essential controls. Repeat with touch and keyboard and verify captions where provided.
- Confirm the gallery can be recreated from that build. Limit supporting material to claims the evidence actually shows. Keep diagnostic copy and secrets out of public routes, captures and bundles.
- Give the entrant the public URL, visual and short rationale, with qualified optional fields. Keep eligibility and form review explicit. Verify the receipt, public listing and complete replay from the submitted listing after the entrant submits.
- Preserve the reviewed build, identifiers and recovery notes through judging. Record the verification time and restore the verified DETOUR release if its host or media changes unexpectedly.
