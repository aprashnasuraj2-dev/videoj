## Whitespace decision

Proceed with **Temporal Lens**: a viewer-pinned rendered frame remains spatially registered over the running film while a seam reveals current video, and the seek rail shows a fixed pin, moving playhead and signed interval between the two times.

The counterexample is important: unrelated camera angles make a wipe noisy and can make the feature look like a gimmick. Therefore the authored judge cue uses Camera A twice with locked geometry: dry spillway around 00:06 versus first flow around 00:14. Manual arbitrary pinning remains supported, but the first-run proof is deliberately matched.
