**Short design description**

AFTERIMAGE turns elapsed time into a spatial layer. A viewer pins one real decoded frame while the film keeps running; the remembered image stays registered to the moving picture, a copper mark remains on the real seek rail, and one seam reveals the past against the present without opening a second player or replacing normal transport.

The Waterline makes the idea readable immediately: the same locked architecture is dry at 00:05 and wet at 00:13. Familiar Video.js v10 controls stay intact, while the comparison adds signed time distance, Pinned / Split / Now views, keyboard and touch operation, captions, reduced motion, mobile reflow and wrapper fullscreen.
